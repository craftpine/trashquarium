# TrashQuarium — How to play in 60 seconds 🐟

> Your desktop aquarium — but the food is… junk files. (Tiếng Việt: [huong-dan-nguoi-moi.md](huong-dan-nguoi-moi.md) · 中文: [how-to-play.zh.md](how-to-play.zh.md))

In the game, click the **?** button in the top right to open this guide any time. Switch language with the 🌐 picker at the top (Tiếng Việt / English / 中文) or in **Settings → Language**.

## 🎁 Getting started

You get **1 guppy at Lv.0** and **40 CBCoin** as a welcome gift. Your fish doesn't beg for food yet, but it loves… files you no longer need.

Turn on **Settings → Desktop aquarium** so your fish swim right under your desktop icons. In a meeting? Turn on **Meeting mode** for slower fish, no chat bubbles and the boat on hold.

## 🗑️ Feed them your junk (safely!)

1. Open the **Feed** tab, pick a fish, then drop files you no longer need into the window.
2. The game checks them first: system folders, files over 4 GiB and content it has already eaten are refused.
3. Files slip into the **Belly**. Nothing is deleted, and **Release** gives a file back. Fed one by mistake? The fish will just feel a little guilty.

Each file gives EXP by size: small files give 20 EXP, and every extra 20 MB adds another 20 EXP.

## 🆙 Growing like a weed

| Level | Your fish is… |
|---|---|
| Lv.0 – 49 | Fry, mostly eating |
| Lv.50 | Juvenile, starting to show off |
| Lv.100 | Adult: done growing, time to think about life |

**10 EXP = 1 level**, so one small file is already 2 levels. Every 5 levels your fish gets a belly like a balloon and **naps for 2 hours** (no feeding meanwhile). Leftover EXP isn't lost; it digests later. Fish never die or leave when you take a break.

## 🐚 Call the boat, grab shells, make bank

- Use the **Shell hunt** tab or the ⛵ quick button on the desktop, then **Call the boat**.
- Watch the swinging claw and press **Space** (Windows) or **Ctrl+Alt+Space** to drop it. Haul a shell back to the boat to get paid.
- White shells are **1 CBCoin**, red **10**, purple **100**. A purple shell is a jackpot.
- Rare shells glow gold and sometimes hide a **pearl** for your collection. You can collect up to 60 shells a day, and new shells appear every 8–22 minutes.

## 🛒 Shopping for the tank

Fish cost **20** to **300 CBCoin**. Cheap fish are tiny, pricey fish are huge: a whale shark (300) is almost 4 times as long as a guppy (20). New fish always start at Lv.0. The tank holds up to **12 fish**, so buy too many and it gets crowded.

## ⛵ Sell or 🥚 breed? (at Lv.100)

**Sell:** press **⛵ Sell** and the boat pays **purchase price × 100 CBCoin** (a 20 CBCoin guppy sells for 2000). Files in the Belly stay safe.

**Breed:** press **Breed**, pick a partner of the **same species** that is also Lv.100, and choose how many eggs.

- Each egg takes one original purchase price off the sale value of **both** parents. The more they breed, the less they sell for, but never less than what you paid.
- Each egg hatches with a **5–20%** chance: cheap fish hatch easily (20%), pricey fish rarely (5%). Eggs that don't hatch still count.
- Fry are born at Lv.0, show which generation they are, and start all over. If the tank fills up, unused eggs are kept.

## 😌 Relax

No real money, no account, no network. Fish never die — they just get a bit lazy sometimes.
